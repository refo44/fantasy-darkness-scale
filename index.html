Scale Criteria

  • Structural Despair
  • Limited Heroism
  • Moral Cynicism
  • Structural Corruption
  • Redemption Difficulty
  • Narrative Acceptance of Injustice
  • Explicit Darkness

Important: this scale does not measure violence alone. A work can contain death, war, and horror without being grimdark if heroism, redemption, and meaningful change remain possible.
