# v2 Scoring Record

Full per-work analysis for every title rescored under the v2 methodology so far. Criteria are
scored 0-4 (see `CRITERIA_THEORY.md` for what each one is a construct of and
`Fantasy_Grimdark_Scale_v2_WIP.xlsx`'s Methodology sheet for the literal 0-4 anchors).
`Final Score = 1.5 + Weighted Internal Score × 2.25`, `Tier = FLOOR(Final Score)`.

Where a work was rescored after initial review (a correction was made mid-discussion), only
the final, corrected scoring is recorded here.

tension rather than pretend it's obvious.
