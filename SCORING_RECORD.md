# v2 Scoring Record

Full per-work analysis for every title rescored under the v2 methodology so far. Criteria are
scored 0-4 (see `CRITERIA_THEORY.md` for what each one is a construct of and
`Fantasy_Grimdark_Scale_v2_WIP.xlsx`'s Methodology sheet for the literal 0-4 anchors).
`Final Score = 1.5 + Weighted Internal Score × 2.25`, `Tier = FLOOR(Final Score)`.

Where a work was rescored after initial review (a correction was made mid-discussion), only
the final, corrected scoring is recorded here.

Flagging this one as a genuine judgment call rather than a clean result: The Little Prince has
a real reputation for poignancy, and its ending is often cited as one of literature's more
touching. But unlike Spirited Away or The Hobbit, where darkness runs across several criteria
at once, this book concentrates almost all of its weight into a single beautiful, sad ending
rather than sustained thematic darkness — so it stays under the tier-3 line even though "sad"
is core to how people remember it. Comfortable calling this tier 2, but wanted to name the
tension rather than pretend it's obvious.
